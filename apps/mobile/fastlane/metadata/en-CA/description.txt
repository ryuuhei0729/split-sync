SwimHub Timer overlays a stopwatch on your swimming race videos and lets you export the result.
Key Features
■ Automatic start signal detection
Analyzes your race video's audio with FFT to automatically detect the start signal (electronic beep or starting gun). Manual fine-tuning is also available.
■ Stopwatch overlay
Displays a stopwatch on the video from the detected start point. Freely customize font size, color, opacity, and position.
■ Lap time management
Add and edit multiple split times. Show turn and finish splits right on the video.
■ High-quality video export
Export in original quality, 1080p, or 720p. Save to your camera roll or share instantly.

Who it's for
- Competitive swimmers who want to review their race footage
- Coaches who want to visualize swimmers' times on video
- Parents who want to keep videos of meets and practice

Privacy
All video processing happens entirely on your device. Your videos and personal data are never sent to external servers.
